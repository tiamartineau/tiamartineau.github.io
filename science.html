My research background is in numerical relativity and black hole-neutron star mergers. I have studied tidal disruption, gravitational waveforms, ejecta composition, and the formation of low-mass accretion disks after merger.

01

Black hole-neutron star mergers

Black hole-neutron star systems are useful laboratories for studying strong-field gravity, neutron-star matter, and the conditions under which compact-object mergers may produce observable electromagnetic counterparts.

My work has focused especially on systems near the neutron-star disruption threshold, where relatively small changes in binary parameters can strongly affect how much matter remains outside the black hole and what that matter looks like.
